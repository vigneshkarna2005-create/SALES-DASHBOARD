# Sales Dashboard (Excel)

Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing sales, profit and regional performance.

## Business Problem
Which regions, categories and products drive sales and profit, and where is the business losing margin?

## Key Insights
1. **Monthly trend:** Sales were highest in October (₹27,056) and lowest in November (about ₹13,000), a drop of roughly 50% in one month. July was also a strong month.
2. **Region:** West (about ₹67,000) and Central (about ₹66,000) led sales. South was the lowest (about ₹42,000), roughly 37% below West.
3. **Category:** Technology was the top category (about ₹93,000, around 39% of total sales), followed by Furniture (about ₹78,000) and Office Supplies (about ₹67,000).
4. **Top products:** Machines (about ₹33,000), Phones and Chairs (about ₹30,000 each) were the top three. Copiers and Furnishings were the lowest (about ₹10,000 each).
5. **Profit:** The overall profit margin is only about 7.8%, which is low for this sales volume.

## Recommendations
- Increase stock and marketing for Technology, especially Machines and Phones
- Investigate why South sales are the lowest and find ways to grow there
- Plan inventory and promotions before the October peak, and find the cause of the November drop
- Review discounts and costs to improve the 7.8% profit margin
- Review the low-selling products (Copiers, Furnishings) for pricing or promotion

## Dashboard Features

## Challenges and Solutions
- **Wrong Net Profit value:** The profit KPI showed ₹0.64, which was incorrect. I corrected the calculation and number formatting, and it now shows ₹18,746.18.
- **Filtered charts:** The charts showed only one region, category and month because slicers were selected. I cleared the filters so the dashboard shows the full data.
- **Monthly trend chart:** I changed the column chart to a line chart so the trend across months is easier to read.
- **Missing regional analysis:** I added a Sales by Region chart.
- **Duplicate slicer:** I removed the repeated Region slicer to keep the layout clean.
